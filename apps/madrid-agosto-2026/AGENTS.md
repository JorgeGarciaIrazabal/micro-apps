## Property-listing criteria

When adding or replacing residential property links in this app, only shortlist listings that meet all of these requirements:

- Price below €300,000.
- More than 110 m² built area.
- 3 or 4 bedrooms.
- At least 2 bathrooms.

A communal pool is optional, not a requirement. Prefer direct, currently active listing URLs over search-result pages, and verify the listing details before editing `src/data.js`.
